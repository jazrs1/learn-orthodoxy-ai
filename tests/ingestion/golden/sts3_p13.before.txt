With Lydia the Recluse: 
On hearing about this saint, a hermit called Lydia, who was an ascetic 
writer, came to see him from Thessalonica  in Greece . She remained for a 
whole year in a cave near -by. She used to meet with Abba Macarius, to have 
the benefit of the guidance and the prayers of this saint . However, she was 
not seen by anyone in the mountain until the day she left the wilderness to go 
back to her country glorifying God.  
His Struggle: 
In the meantime, St. Macarius  strove to humiliate himself more than all 
the other hermits, proclaiming that he was the lowest among them . Hearing 
that some monks refrained from eating any cooked food during Lent ; he did 
the same and sustained himself by vegetables for seven years , and  
understanding that a monk kept crumbles of bread in a pitcher, from which he 
ate only what his hand would get through its narrow opening; he decided to 
follow suit . If he would hear  that someone was practicing a specific virtue 
that he did not try, he would not have peace until he would master it more 
than the others .Once, he decided to overcome sleep, so he refrained from 
entering into his cell for ten consecutive days and nights without any rest.
