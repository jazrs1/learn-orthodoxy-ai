The governor ordered beating ‘Begol’ the soldier on his head with rods until blood flowed from his mouth and nose. He also ordered whipping him 200 times at intervals to lengthen the period of his suffering. As to the priest ‘Begol,’ he also suffered much that so many believers wept for his sake. The two saints were then brought back to prison, to the astonishment of the other prisoners who thought that they were already dead due to their severe tortures.

Martyrdom of Begol the Priest:

On the 15th of Amshir, after this saintly priest endured so many tortures by governor ‘Armanius;’ his head was finally cut off. Right before that, he was joyfully crying out: “Now my joy is complete, my priesthood is consummated. For forty years, I served the Lord for the sake of this moment.” Then, he was martyred by the sword.

Begol the Soldier sent to Ansena:

After the martyrdom of ‘Begol,’ the priest, Governor ‘Armanius’ placed ‘Begol’ the soldier in a bronze pot over a fire. The saint praying for help and making the sign of the Cross on himself, the angel of the Lord came and rescued him. The governor then sent him by boat to ‘Ansena’ in Upper Egypt. When the ship arrived at his town, the saint called on his sister ‘Theodora,’ advised her to keep praying, and doing mercy to the poor and the fatherless, then he bid her farewell. In ‘Ansena,’ the governor ‘Ariana’ ordered to have the saint pressed by the hinbazeen, to take his fingernails off and to beat him with iron rods. In all that, the Lord strengthened him ultimately. Then, he was hanged upside down on the sail of a boat and was whipped. The next day, he was brought from prison to be cast in a fiery furnace. The Archangel ‘Michael’ came, freed him, and brought him to the place where ‘Ariana’ the governor was sitting. On seeing that, one of the soldiers by the name of ‘Kolta’ proclaimed his faith and was martyred on the 26th of Baramhat.

Sent to Diocletian:

The saint was then forwarded to Emperor ‘Diocletian’ in Antioch, along with a complete report on his case. The emperor started to torture the saint, and he killed the little servant ‘Sergius’ who was accompanying him. Then, he put the saint in prison, where St. ‘Victor’ (Boctor), the son of ‘Romanius,’ the high official visited him. As God granted the saint to perform miracles in prison, in anger, the emperor ordered to cast him in a dark dungeon, put a heavy rock on his chest, and leave him to die. In the morning, the soldiers found the saint spreading his arms like a cross and praying. They brought him to the emperor, who gave his orders to cut his head off to receive the crown of martyrdom on the 13th of Pashons.
